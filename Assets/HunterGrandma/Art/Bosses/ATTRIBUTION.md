# Stage boss art sources

Selected by the user's Notion reference: https://app.notion.com/p/4047313398e081708791ed0d9751c261
Downloaded originals are preserved under `Source/`; gameplay extracts a selected frame into transparent 64×64 `Frames/` assets with point filtering. No replacement AI artwork is used. White background/grid pixels are excluded from the worker frame. All seven source pages listed CC0 when checked on 2026-10-26; NPC Dancers offers several licenses and this project selects CC0. Credits are retained voluntarily.
